# Operator runbook

Install and migrate explicitly with `DATABASE_URL=... ./scripts/migrate.sh`; startup is nondestructive. Configure a strong JWT secret, leave generated/provider routes quarantined, and never expose the unauthenticated ingest path outside a mutually authenticated gateway.

Telemetry needs consent, schema/source identity, and monotonic device sequence. Safety scores are advisory. Dispatch requires independent human review and a receipt; resolution requires outcome evidence. Insurance, dashcam, telematics, SMS, emergency, and vehicle hardware actions fail closed without credentials and signed contracts. Qualified safety/dispatch professionals retain authority.
